Support

2026-10-16

Skyforge Ace requires an Apple Watch running watchOS 10 or later and plays independently on the watch. Steer with the Digital Crown or drag across the battlefield. Firing is automatic. Tap the battlefield to use your ability when charged. Tap the circular pause button or long-press the battlefield to pause.

Play with five planes and a three-round sortie. Progress, coins, unlocks, settings, the sortie checkpoint, and the adaptive pilot profile are saved locally. Removing the app can lose this progress; Apple backups may restore it separately.

No ads, in-app purchases, or subscriptions. Apple processes any App Store purchase or refund. For a purchase or refund issue, use Apple’s support at https://support.apple.com/billing.

For help or privacy requests, email Semih Gülücü (Miniverse Games) at miniverse.games1@gmail.com. Include your Apple Watch model, watchOS version, and the steps that led to the issue. Screenshots are optional; remove personal information before sending. Never send passwords or payment card details.
