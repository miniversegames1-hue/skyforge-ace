Privacy Policy

2026-10-16

Skyforge Ace is developed by Semih Gülücü (Miniverse Games). For privacy questions or support, email miniverse.games1@gmail.com. This policy covers the game and this support website.

The game runs independently on Apple Watch with watchOS 10 or later. It has no accounts, network features, analytics, advertising, tracking, in-app purchases, subscriptions, or third-party SDKs. It does not access health, location, contacts, camera, or microphone data.

Game progress, coins, unlocks, settings, a sortie flight checkpoint, and an adaptive pilot skill and difficulty profile are saved on your device using UserDefaults. The profile adjusts gameplay locally; it is not cloud machine learning. The game does not send this information to the developer or store it in a developer cloud.

Deleting the app removes its local data. Apple-managed backups are separate and may restore saved data when the app is restored. Emailing the developer does not delete data on your device or in Apple backups.

If you choose to email support, we receive your sender address, message, and any attachments you send. We use these only to reply, troubleshoot, and improve the game, not for advertising or sale. We retain support correspondence only as needed for your request and applicable legal obligations. Contact the email above to request access or deletion where applicable; avoid sending unnecessary personal or sensitive information.

Apple may share aggregate App Store information and diagnostics with developers under Apple settings, including user opt-in settings where applicable. Apple handles App Store purchases and refunds; we do not receive your payment card information. Apple services and backups are governed by Apple’s privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may receive standard request information, including IP addresses and request logs, under its own privacy policy: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement. This site has no forms, cookies, or analytics code.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent personal information through support, contact us to request its deletion. We may update this policy when the game or our practices change; the date above identifies the current version.
